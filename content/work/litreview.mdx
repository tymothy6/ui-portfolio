summary: >-
  This plan outlines a methodical approach and establishes metrics for
  evaluating a product in assisting biomedical researchers, focusing on journey
  mapping and alignment with the aim to simplify literature search and
  selection.
overview: >-
  In this user research plan, I provide a roadmap to evaluate a tool's
  proficiency in aiding scientists to identify optimal treatment responses. I've
  pinpointed the primary audience and delve into the motivations of scientists
  in biomedical research. Ideal journey mapping is key, shedding light on user
  experiences and revealing opportunities for improvement. By mapping user
  tasks, I propose a framework to evaluate navigation, comprehension, and
  satisfaction with desired features. These metrics ensure that my research not
  only pinpoints user challenges but also aligns with the product's ultimate
  goal: simplifying the literature search and selection process with minimal
  consultation of external tools. With a focused testing strategy on definitive
  usage moments, I intend to refine the tool's alignment with user expectations.
  Finally, I describe a strategy to balance research insights with specific
  success metrics and constraints.
